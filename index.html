About Me

I am Leslie Mae B. Cerilo, currently in my second year at UPOU under the AADDA program. I balance my time as a student with the responsibilities I take on as a leader in my local church. I try to find my passion through learning and serving at the same time. That is why I am also part of our media ministry.

One of my dreams is to create films and be part of a production team whose primary goal is to create meaningful work with purpose.
